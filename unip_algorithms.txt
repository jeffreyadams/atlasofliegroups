Algorithms for Arthur (special) unipotent representations in atlas-scripts
(written 2026-10-21, branch fpp_unitary)

alt_fast vs gentau (both in atlas-scripts/unipotent_alt.at)
===========================================================

They compute the same set; they differ only in how a candidate is decided.

Shared:
  - Orbits: the dual orbits O that are fixed by delta^vee and have a real form
    for G. unipotent_orbits_gentau(G) is exactly the list alt_fast uses.
  - Candidates: gamma = O.H/2; the irreducibles q at gamma with tau(q) = S,
    S = integral simple roots nonsingular on gamma (the cheap necessary
    condition).

alt_fast (unipotent_representations_alt_fast):
  q is unipotent iff GK_dim_claude(q) = GK_dim_maximal_ideal_memo(G,gamma).
  This is the definition Ann(q) = J_max(gamma): every annihilator at gamma
  lies in J_max, so equality is the same as equal GK dimension. Each candidate
  is tested independently, using the block-memoized GK dimension.

gentau (unipotent_representations_gentau(G,O)):
  q is unipotent iff it has the same generalized tau invariant as psph, the
  spherical rep of the *split* form at gamma:
  same_PI_step(q, TalphabetaTauss_bitset(psph)) (paramRep.at).
  Each test also decides others:
    - if the test fails, every candidate visited by its search fails
      (they all have the same GK dimension);
    - if it succeeds, the visited candidates with q's tau invariant succeed;
    - every decided candidate decides its images under the torus twists
      (gentau_twist_xs) and the contragredient, which preserve unipotence.

Which is used:
  - unipotent_representations(G) goes through unip_hook (arthur_hash.at), which
    uses the stored data (exceptional F4_s .. E8_q; A_to_8_unip.at,
    C_to_8_unip.at, spin_to_8_unip.at) when it can. Otherwise
    unipotent_representations_compute calls gentau (unip_gentau_flag = true,
    default since 2026-10-21), or alt_fast if unip_gentau_flag = false and
    unip_alt_fast_flag = true (it was the default before that).
  - gentau was added Oct 20-21 (a6c55dd4, 7c68fde7) to reach groups where
    alt_fast was too slow. According to the headers of C_to_8_unip.at and
    spin_to_8_unip.at, it produced the stored data for Sp(14,R) and Sp(16,R),
    and for so(11,6), so(10,7), so(9,8), so(8,8), so(10,6), so(9,7);
    everything else there came from alt_fast.

Speed / agreement:
  - Same answers on every form tested, to rank 7.
  - gentau is 2-4x faster at rank 7, except so(11,4) (0.8x).
  - Most of gentau's time is in same_PI_step. The twist propagation was worth
    30x on one Sp(16,R) orbit (4.05 h -> 494 s).

Exact count: unipotent_count(G,O) (unipotent_alt.at)
====================================================

  sum over tau in the family F of W(rdi) of
      [tau : Ind_{W_sing}^{W(rdi)} triv] * [tau : M]

  gamma = O.H/2, rdi = integrality_datum(G,gamma),
  W_sing = W(roots of rdi singular on gamma),
  M = the coherent continuation representation on the span of the irreducibles
      at gamma + 2 rho (coherent_continuation_character), computed on standard
      modules: filter by Cartans (Cayley transforms go to more split ones);
      modulo more split Cartans w.I(p) = eps(w,p) I(w x p), eps = (-1)^#(positive
      imaginary roots of rdi made negative by w). So trace(w) = sum of eps(w,p)
      over p with w x p = p. No KL polynomials or cells. (Formula as in
      coherent_characters.at, there for the full W.)
  F = the family of Springer(d(O_i)) = sgn x Springer(O_i), where O_i is O
      seen in the integral dual datum.
  Conventions checked directly: Springer(principal) = trivial, and the
  finite-dimensional cell carries sign.

  - Theorems (Adams, Mason-Brown, Ionov, Vogan preprint): alt_fast and the
    induction procedure below both find all Arthur unipotent reps. gentau has
    no theorem (except in type A), so it ends each orbit with an assert
    #ans = unipotent_count(G,O) that runs even with seat_belt_on false.
  - M is cached by (G, gamma%1) (unipotent_count_M_cache).
  - Standard-module M = cell M (the old method, kept as
    coherent_continuation_character_cells in unipotent_count.at) on 19 groups,
    92 cosets of gamma (SL(2,R) .. E6_s, Sp(4,C), Sp(3,1), so*(8)).
  - Cost: whole count for all orbits F4_s 1.1 s, E6_q 2.7 s, E6_s 1.9 s.
    E8_s: 1109 s for the first orbit (#41 E8(a7), building M: 526471
    params, 199952 involutions), under 1 GB; then 0.3 s per orbit in the same
    coset. The cell version passed 26 GB on E8_s and was stopped.
  - Agrees with alt_fast orbit by orbit on 25 groups up to rank 6 (E6_q, E6_s,
    SO(6,6), Sp(10,R), ...). [tau : Ind triv] was never > 1 on the family.
  - Harnesses: unipotent_count.at.
  - Renamed from arthur_count: arthur_hash.at already defines a global
    arthur_count = [int].

Induction from the Bala-Carter Levi: unipotent_induced.at
=========================================================

unipotent_representations_induced(G,O):
  - L^vee = the Bala-Carter Levi of O; L = the dual Levi of G.
  - Run over every Levi L'_R of G (G included) and, inside it, every Levi L_R
    of complex type L.
  - For each distinguished H_L of L^vee saturating to O, take the unipotent
    reps of L_R at H_L/2 - rho(u). theta_induce adds rho(u); real induction
    preserves the infinitesimal character.
  - Induce to L'_R, then to G; keep every irreducible with nonzero
    coefficient.

  Flag unipotent_induced_theta_first:
    true (default): theta-stable L'_R in G, then real L_R in L'_R;
      10-20% faster in every timing run, as expected.
    false: real L'_R in G, then theta-stable L_R in L'_R.

  Completeness is a theorem (Adams, Mason-Brown, Ionov, Vogan preprint).
  Soundness for every q and every range: a constituent has AV inside
  Ind_L^G d_L(O_L) = d(O), which forces Ann = J_max. The comparison with
  alt_fast / unipotent_count is a consistency check.

  - induced = alt_fast = unipotent_count, orbit by orbit, on 31 groups up to
    rank 6, including SO_star(8), SL(3,H), Sp(4,C). Both orders give
    identical sets.
  - Speed: about equal to alt_fast up to rank 4; 3-8x slower at rank 6
    (E6_q 187 s vs 24 s). Not profiled yet.
  - Not tested on E7.

Real forms of a Levi (unipotent_induced.at, added 2026-10-21)
-------------------------------------------------------------
  real_forms_of_Levi(G,S): one x in KGB(G) per real form L_R of the standard
    Levi S, up to K-conjugacy. involution(x) is distinguished for L, i.e. the
    Cartan is fundamental in L_R. x and y are identified when
    y = cross(n,x), n in N_W(L). The search uses W(L)^theta (generators s,
    st, sts on theta-orbits of simple roots) plus Howlett's moves
    nu = w_0(K) w_0(K meet S'). Check: S = {} gives the number of Cartan
    classes (Sp(4,R) 4, Sp(6,R) 6, F4_s 8).
  real_forms_of_Levi(G,S,false): W(L)^theta only, one x per
    (L_R, chamber of Z(L)). This is David's original scheme (all x with a
    distinguished involution, up to W(L)^theta), and it is what induction
    needs: q depends on the chamber of H+. The N_W(L) moves were my addition
    and lost reps when used for induction (Sp(4,R) H=[2,0]: 6 of 8).
  Levi_pair(x,S,theta_first): H+- = (1 +- ^theta) H_c, H_c = the central
    coweight of L. L' = centralizer of H+ (theta_first) or of H-; L =
    centralizer of both. One from_dominant of M*H_outer + H_inner puts L' and
    L in standard position together.
  unipotent_representations_induced now uses these; the old enumeration is
    unipotent_representations_induced_by_parabolics.
  Results: correct on 23 groups (7 small ones plus 16 timed, up to E6_s).
    Faster than the old enumeration on SU(3,3) and SU(4,2) (1.7-2x),
    10-13% on E6, about equal elsewhere.
  Profile, E6_s: 103 s total. 89% is the inductions themselves (317
    inductions yield 68 reps, about 290 ms each), 10% unipotent_at on the
    Levis, 0.2% the enumeration.
