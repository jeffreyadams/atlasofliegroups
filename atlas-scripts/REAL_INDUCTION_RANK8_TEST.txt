REAL INDUCTION IN FPP: HOW TO TEST IN RANK 8
============================================
(1 Oct 2026, David's laptop, Claude. Code: to_ht_branch_alt commits d569309d, 4478c707,
a33d48a3, 0ba1be2c. For a Claude session on a big machine.)

WHAT THE CODE DOES
------------------
A hermitian p may be (a constituent of) Ind_{P'}^G(J(pM')) from a maximal real Levi M'
with nu trivial on the split center of M'. If the induction is IRREDUCIBLE, then
  - p is unitary iff pM' is, iff every simple derived factor of pM' is unitary;
  - the hermitian form of p is real_induce_standard(form of pM', G), exactly;
  - to height HT: if Ind(J(pM')) and J(p) agree up to height HT, the form of p to HT
    is to_ht(that induced form, HT).
Pieces (FPP_lambdas.at unless noted):
  ind_cand_params_lin_tau(p) -> (p0,[(pM',tau)]): candidates from linear algebra on
     restricted roots (David's construction). Settings icp_lin_K (max Levis),
     icp_lin_b (branching), icp_lin_nodes, icp_lin_maxdepth (negative d = dim a + d),
     icp_lin_check (validation, costs ~10%). Default K=1, b=1: cheapest.
  real_ind_irr_suff(p0,tau) and (p0,tau,HT): David's sufficient conditions for
     irreducibility: no coroot outside M' with av*gamma, av*theta*gamma integers of
     strictly opposite sign (to HT: only coroots passing test() of kl.at at HT).
  ind_cand_params(p): the older W-orbit search. Hopeless in E7/E8 (|W| ~ 2.9e6 / 7e8);
     do not use there.
Hooks (FPP_faces_herm.at), all OFF by default; answers identical when on:
  real_ind_vertex_flag   screen in localFD_1Dirac2_by_zero: an irreducibly induced
                         vertex is labelled unitary/nonunitary from the derived factors
                         of pM' (umatch/numatch); nonunitary vertices are dropped.
                         Settings real_ind_vertex_K/b/maxdepth (try maxdepth -2).
                         Report: real_ind_vertex_report().
  real_ind_form_flag     in big_unitary_hash (add_param_inf, add_param, *_interrupt):
                         store the induced form instead of computing the form of p.
                         Report: real_ind_form_report().
  real_ind_flag          older status() hook; superseded, leave off.

VERIFIED (laptop, rank <= 4 unless said)
----------------------------------------
- Candidates validated: real_induce_standard(pM',G) equivalent to p, every time.
- Signature identity (irreducible case): 905/905 exact, 9 groups.
- Irreducibility criterion: 0 counterexamples in 1384 cases (+40 E7_s x_open, checked by
  real_induce_irreducible); to-HT version: 1317/1317 truncated forms exact.
- herm_center is now alcove_center (built-in); Levi Params may go into FPP hashes.

MEASURED SO FAR (laptop; answers identical in every run)
--------------------------------------------------------
E7_s x_open, one_level driver, params_to_openE7.at preloaded:
                     off      vertex screen (maxdepth -2)   form hook (K=1)
  lambda = rho       300 s    357 s                          370 s
  lambda #5          31.8 s   48.9 s                         33.9 s
  lambda #7          20.7 s   29.4 s                         31.5 s
- Vertex screen at rho: drops 324 of 10825 vertices (3%); edges -3.6%; net of its own
  cost it saves <= ~3%. Its cost is ~6 ms per vertex, mostly the candidate search.
- Form hook at rho: removes 357 E7 tests worth only 6.6 s (~18 ms each) but adds 334 full
  forms on M' (SO(6,6).GL(1)) worth 47.5 s. Forms on M' are computed whole even for to_ht.
- Spin(7,5) x_open: +1 to +4%. The expensive tests there have no candidates at all.
Lesson so far: the parameters with real-induction structure are the ones the existing
machinery already handles cheaply. Rank 8 is the test of whether that changes.

WHAT TO TEST IN RANK 8
----------------------
Groups: E8_s and E8_q at x_open (equal rank); Spin(8,8) and Spin(9,7) if time allows
(spinUnipReps.at covers only p+q <= 14, so their unipotents get computed: slow).
For each group:
1. Pick a few lambda in FPP_lambdas(x_open(G)) with
   is_pos_on_LKTs(parameter(x_open(G),lambda,0*lambda)); start with ones you expect to be
   cheap, NOT rho. Never isolate a lambda from late in a sweep (memory blows up: see
   ATLAS_NOTES section 6).
2. Arms, side by side, same load (ATLAS_NOTES section 8 caps: 1/5 of cores, 1/3 memory):
     off:     (nothing)
     screen:  real_ind_vertex_flag := true
              real_ind_vertex_maxdepth := -2
     form:    real_ind_form_flag := true      (icp_lin_K := 1, icp_lin_b := 1 default)
   Use the driver and preloads that are standard on that machine for E8 (e.g.
   edges_F_E_new.at plus E8cohIndPlusPlushc.at) and keep them identical across arms.
3. After the run, print in the SAME process:
     RUNTIME; count of uhash params with p.x = x_open(G) (must agree across arms);
     the FPP-unitary face line; FDcount (vertex and edge counts); real_ind_vertex_report();
     real_ind_form_report(); and the tested tables (tested_pos_hash, tested_neg_hash and
     the *_to_ht_hashes with their times), one line per test with kind, ms, real form,
     param. A driver that does all this: scratch e7/r_*.in on the laptop, i.e.
       <FPP_globalDirac.at  ... preloads ...
       set G = E8_s
       set xo = x_open(G)
       set lam = FPP_lambdas(xo)[i]
       real_ind_vertex_flag := true      {or the other arm}
       set t0 = elapsed_ms()
       FPP_unitary_hash_one_level([(xo,lam)])   {or the standard E8 driver}
       prints("RUNTIME ",elapsed_ms()-t0)
       real_ind_vertex_report(); real_ind_form_report(); prints("FDcount ",FDcount)
       prints("INRANGE ",count(for p in big_unitary_hash.uhash(G).list() do p.x = xo od))
       for q@i in tested_pos_hash.list() do prints("T pos ",tested_pos_times[i]," ",q) od
       ... same for tested_neg_hash and the to_ht hashes ...
4. Compare tests between arms by parameter: total ms of tests that disappear with the hook
   versus ms of new tests (forms on M' show up as new tests on Levi real forms). That
   difference, not the runtime alone, says whether the idea can pay.
5. Worth checking in rank 8 before trusting anything: a sample of real_ind_irr_suff=true
   cases against real_induce_irreducible(q,G) = 1*p (expensive in E8: sample a few dozen),
   and that the in-range unitary counts agree between arms.

What would make it pay (not done): computing forms on M' only to the needed height, or
from the simple derived factors of pM' (memoized, much smaller) glued together, which
needs glue_sigs for disconnected M' (cohom_reductionND.at handles connected M' only).
