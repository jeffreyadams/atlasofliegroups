On of the subtle points of the theory is the definition of the generic
degree of a representation of the Weyl group. The important notion of
special representation is defined in terms of this: the generic degree
and another degree (I think it is called the fake degree, or just
degree) are equal is the defining property of special. There is a
complicated algorithm in geck_generic.at to compute generic degree,
which is inductive in nature.

We also compute generic degree at least one other way: I think every
weyl group representation is contained in a "family" of weyl group
representations, which contains one special representation, the the
reps in the family all have the same generic degree. So you can
compute the generic degree by finding this family and identifying the
special. For classical groups this is given by an algorithm, and for
exceptional groups in tables.
